Grilled Chicken & Veggies
  • Chicken breast — 200g
  • Zucchini — 1
  • Bell pepper — 1/2
  • Olive oil — 1 tbsp
  • Salt & pepper — to taste
  • Season chicken and veggies.
  • Grill chicken 5–6 min each side.
  • Grill veggies until tender.

Lactose-Free Berry Oat Smoothie
  • Lactose-free milk — 1 cup
  • Berries — 1/2 cup
  • Oats — 2 tbsp
  • Honey (optional) — 1 tsp
  • Add all to blender.
  • Blend 40 seconds.
  • Serve chilled.
