Banana Oat Pancake

Details

Dessert - A healthy Gluten-free recipe.

Ingredients

  • Banana – 1 mashed
  • Baking powder – 1/2 tsp
  • Gluten-free oats – 1/2 cup
  • Almond Milk – 1/2 cup

Instructions

  1. Mix ingredients.
  2. Bake for 10 minutes.
  3. Let it cool.
