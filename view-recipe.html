Oats Banana Pancake

Creator

[Sara]

Details

Dessert - A healthy Gluten-free recipe.

Ingredients

  • Gluten-free oats – 1/2 cup
  • Banana – 1 mashed
  • Almond milk – 1/2 cup
  • Baking powder – 1/2 tsp

Instructions

  1. Mix all ingredients together.
  2. Heat a non-stick pan on medium.
  3. Cook 2–3 minutes per side.
  4. Serve warm.
