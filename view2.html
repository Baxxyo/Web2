Lactose-Free Smoothie

Creator

Maha

Details

A refreshing lactose-free smoothie made with fruits and lactose-free milk. Perfect for a light snack.

Ingredients

  • Lactose-free milk — 1 cup
  • Berries — 1/2 cup
  • Oats — 2 tbsp
  • Honey (optional) — 1 tsp

Instructions

  1. Add all ingredients to a blender.
  2. Blend for 40 seconds.
  3. Serve chilled.
