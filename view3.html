Grilled Chicken & Veggies

Creator

leen

Details

A healthy grilled chicken meal with mixed vegetables. High in protein and perfect for a balanced diet.

Ingredients

  • Chicken breast — 200g
  • Zucchini — 1
  • Bell pepper — 1/2
  • Olive oil — 1 tbsp
  • Salt & pepper — to taste

Instructions

  1. Season chicken and veggies.
  2. Grill chicken 5–6 minutes on each side.
  3. Grill veggies until tender.
  4. Serve hot.
